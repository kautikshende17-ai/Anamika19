Dear Anamika,

Happy birthday. In a world overrun by rushed work, automated noise, and loud distractions, your deliberate taste, eye for craftsmanship, and quiet dedication to making things look and feel right are rare qualities.


Whether we're dissecting museum artifacts, pacing out design systems, or navigating complex life priorities, you have been an exceptional creative partner and a steady anchor.


May this year bring you unbroken creative agency, projects that respect your talents, and the quiet confidence to guard your peace without hesitation.


— Kautik
